6. Data Subjects' Rights

Data subjects may exercise the following rights under the GDPR, free of charge and within one month of the request:

  1. Right of access — to obtain confirmation of whether their personal data are processed and to receive a copy.
  2. Right to rectification — to have inaccurate personal data corrected without undue delay.
  3. Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”) — to have personal data deleted under the conditions of Article 17.
  4. Right to restriction of processing — to restrict processing in the cases set out in Article 18.
  5. Right to data portability — to receive their data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  6. Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interest or for direct marketing.
  7. Right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  8. Right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority {{ supervisory_authority }}.

9. Data Breaches

In the event of a personal data breach, the Controller shall assess the risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects without undue delay. A breach that is likely to result in a risk to those rights and freedoms shall be notified to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk. Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk, affected data subjects shall be informed without undue delay.

All breaches, including near misses, are recorded in the internal breach register maintained by {{ breach_register_owner }}, documenting the facts, effects and remedial measures taken.
