1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 Purpose.  This manual defines the food safety management system based on the Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) principles of Codex Alimentarius, applicable to the activities performed at {{ site_name }}, {{ site_address }}.

1.2 Scope.  It covers all stages of the process from {{ process_scope_start }} to {{ process_scope_end }}, including receiving, storage, preparation, production, packaging and dispatch of the products described in section 3.

1.3 Responsibility.  All personnel are required to comply with the procedures in this manual. The HACCP team leader is responsible for maintaining and updating the system; deviations must be reported without delay.

8. Verification and Review

8.1 Verification.  The HACCP system is verified through {{ verification_methods }}, including calibration of monitoring equipment, review of CCP records and microbiological or chemical testing performed by {{ testing_laboratory }}.

8.2 Review.  The plan is reviewed by the HACCP team at least {{ review_frequency }}, and whenever changes occur in raw materials, recipes, equipment, packaging, premises or legal requirements.

8.3 Non-conformities.  Any deviation from critical limits is treated as a non-conformity: the affected product is placed on hold, corrective action is applied and the event is recorded and reviewed by the HACCP team leader.
